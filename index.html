璇璣圖 Star Gauge

Su Hui wove this square of silk in the 4th century for a husband posted far away, and drifting from her. Its 841 characters read across, down and diagonally, forwards and backwards — one cloth holding thousands of poems.

Trace a thread through the grid — seven characters a line, four lines to a quatrain.

Traces four lines and stops on the finished quatrain. A demonstration path, not a documented historical reading — touch the grid at any time to take it over.

About this poem

Her name was Su Hui (蘇蕙); his was Dou Tao (竇滔). She wove in five colours of silk, the colours marking different ways the cloth could be read. The poems hidden in it are usually counted at nearly three thousand, and they turn on longing, separation and constancy. The centre character, 心 ("heart"), belongs to every reading and to none.
